HOW THIS WORKS

READ-ONLY

This app only reads public files. Nothing here can change the archive, and nothing you do is recorded.

NOTHING COLLECTED

No accounts, no tracking, no uploads, no cookies. Everything runs in your browser and stops when you close the tab.

VERIFY IT YOURSELF

Images are fingerprinted on your own machine and checked against the record. You don't have to trust the result — you can watch it happen.

OPEN AND ADAPTABLE

The archive, its media, and this app are all public and openly licensed. Any artist is free to build the same for their own work.
